# 3 directions dealt for this brief

Choose the one whose way of composing can best carry an idea from your inventory (SKILL.md steps 1 and 4), not the
most familiar one. Then deal it in full with the same command plus `--direction <id>` (and without --options).

## `dark-room` — The dark room
Idea engine: One dark field from top to bottom, and in it, lit things. Photographs and objects appear like exhibits in a dim gallery: each with space around it, each the brightest thing on its screen.
Composition: A single near-black or deep-toned ground runs the full page with no section bands. First screen: either the name at full width in a light weight along the bottom edge with one dim photograph above it, or one object or photograph centred at 40–50% of the viewport with a line of text beneath at 16–18px.

## `one-motif` — One motif
Idea engine: Take one shape from the subject and let it run the whole system. A drilling tool's octagonal cross-section becomes the buttons, the image masks and the loader.
Composition: Hero: the motif appears once at enormous scale as a flat filled shape, cropped by the viewport (a circle 120vh across entering from the right, a chamfered block filling the lower half), with a short headline at 6–8vw and one supporting sentence set on the remaining ground, left-aligned on a shared margin. Through the page the motif returns as: the mask for every photograph (via `clip-path` or `border-radius` made from the same geometry), the shape of the primary button, the bullet beside list items at text size, and one full-width band that separates the two halves of the page.

## `name-as-monument` — The name as a monument
Idea engine: The name is the headline. Set it so large that it becomes architecture: as wide as the viewport, cropped by an edge, or stacked to fill the screen.
Composition: First screen: the wordmark spans 88–100% of the viewport width (font-size 16–24vw, set with a clamp so it never wraps), anchored either to the bottom edge with its descenders nearly touching it, or dead centre with everything else pushed to the corners. One line of supporting text at 16–20px sits in a corner on a shared margin; navigation is three or four words in the opposite corner.
