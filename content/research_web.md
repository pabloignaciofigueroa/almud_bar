## 3. Facts (each with source)

- **Address:** Serrano 325, Castro, Isla de Chiloé, Chile (S1). Wanderlog/kupi: "Al frente del Centro Cultural de Castro, Ignacio Serrano 325, 5700000 Castro, Los Lagos, Chile" (S5, S12, S10). carta.menu postal code: 5700552 (S9).
- **Location:** about one block from the Plaza de Armas: "just a block away from the main square" (S5). La Tercera says it is one block from the plaza (S8). A 2015 reviewer wrote "A una cuadra de la plaza de Castro" (S3).
- **Coordinates:** -42.4826, -73.76176; OSM way 273067682; Plus code 4798G68Q+X7 (S13).
- **Phone:** +56 65 253 0084 (S1, S2, S9).
- **Email:** baralmud@gmail.com (S1).
- **Website / digital menu:** https://www.qrcarta.com/restaurant/castro/almud-bar/1755/?type=carta&id_carta=3362 (S5). kupi lists only "www.qrcarta.com" (S12).
- **Hours. Sources disagree; confirm with the bar:**
  - Wanderlog (S5): Sun "Closed"; Mon "Closed"; Tue "6PM–12AM"; Wed "6PM–12AM"; Thu "6PM–1AM"; Fri "12–1AM, 6PM–2AM"; Sat "12–2AM, 6PM–2AM". The Fri/Sat lines are as displayed and look garbled.
  - kupi (S12): opens daily at 18:00. Closes at midnight Mon–Wed, 01:00 Thu, 02:00 Fri–Sat. Closed Sunday.
  - carta.menu (S9): Mon–Thu 18:00–00:00; Fri–Sat 18:00–01:00; Sunday not listed.
  - Tripadvisor: "No hay horarios disponibles" (S2). A 2017 English review: "opens around 7:30" (S1).
- **Owners:** "run by the talented architect Carmen Paz Oyarzun and her partner Sylvan" (S5). La Tercera, Aug 2024: Carmen Paz Oyarzun, "arquitecta chilota", and her partner Sylvan (S8). Reviews from 2015–2016 mention the owners serving in person: "Atendido por su propios dueños" (MauricioUG, Jan 2015) and "atendido por la dueña" (oscar mendoza, Sep 2016) (S2).
- **Founding:** the earliest Tripadvisor reviews are from Sep 2014 (S2). La Tercera (28 Aug 2024): "Almud Bar es el bar que acaba de cumplir 10 años y una parada obligada si estás en Chiloé." (S8). Wanderlog: "celebrating a decade of existence" (S5). Both are consistent with an opening in 2014.
- **Name:** Lonely Planet: "named after Chiloé's unit of measurement for potatoes" (S11). A reviewer in 2015: "Es un lugar nuevo, cuenta con una decoración original, usando la unidad de medida chilota, el almud…" (Seo2, S2). Another: "Llegue sin saber que era un Almud y me encante con los que decoran el lugar" (Pancha Z, S2). In other words, wooden almud measures are part of the decor.
- **Open year-round:** "Se trata de uno de esos locales, con ambiente, barra y terraza, que no cierra en ninguna época del año" (S8).
- **Concept:** "Lo que hace imbatible a este lugar es su carta de coctelería de autor" (S8). "Cocteles que en Almud Bar puedes probar acompañado de una carta de picoteos, sánguches y pizzas artesanales." (S8)
- **Tripadvisor attributes (S2):** Cocina: Bar, Pub, Chilena. Comidas: Cena, Bebidas. Dietas: Apto para vegetarianos, Opciones veganas, Opciones sin gluten. Características: Acceso para silla de ruedas, Acepta tarjetas de crédito, Asiento, Bar completo, Servicio de mesa, Sirve alcohol, Wi-Fi gratis.
- **Payment:** a 2015 review says "se puede pagar con red compra" (S3, javierajos_o / Javiera Ortiz).
- **Lonely Planet (S11):** "The best proper bar in Castro – named after Chiloé's unit of measurement for potatoes – offers a wide range of cocktails (CH$3000 to CH$7500), craft beers…" (cut off at source; undated, probably c. 2015–2018).
- **Menu changes:** reviews in 2019 praise a "nueva carta" (Sergio R: "Unas de las cosas que más me gustan del Almud es que cambian constantemente la carta,"; Vane Miranda: "Excelente nueva carta", Dec 2019) (S2).
- **Early drink-list size:** "con uno de sus más de 60 cócteles" (_donNatanael, Sep 2014) (S2). "Piscolas XL" were a signature in 2015–2017 (several reviews, S2).
- **Events (Tiki Night, Fondita, DJs):** **nothing found outside Instagram.** One 2015 review title reads "Excelente bar, buena música en vivo" (S3), which suggests occasional live music.
- **Awards/rankings:** Tripadvisor #1 of 6 bars in Castro (S1). Evendo #8 of 10 in Chiloé (S10). Wanderlog #11 dinner spots in Castro (S7). No formal awards found.

### 4a. La Tercera, 28 Aug 2024 (S8). Names exact; ingredients summarized by the fetch tool, not verbatim. Prices CLP.
**Coctelería de autor**
- **A veces me pongo Coquette** — $6.500 — pisco infused with berries (frutos del bosque), whisky cream, raspberry, orange, cinnamon syrup (described as fairly sweet).
- **Qué te poní encachao** — $6.900 — Ballantine's Wild whisky, cherry, dry vermouth, flor de jamaica (hibiscus) syrup, raspberry meringue.
- **Gin Mawünko** (with premium tonic) — $7.900 — gin from Archipiélago Destilería (near Chonchi), secret maceration of endemic island botanicals.

**Local spirits**
- **Vodka Sirena** — $5.700 (short serve) — made from Chiloé potatoes and spring water. The article says it has won eight medals, including a double gold in New York.

**Food**
- **Greditas** — base of baked diced potatoes (no price given).
- **Camarón que se duerme** — $10.900 — shrimp, cream cheese, chives, cilantro, ginger, mushrooms.
- **Tutukulito Sakayama** — $12.900 — marinated tuna loin tataki, bread, house sauce.
- **La Carne es débil** — $12.900 — beef cut with mushrooms and cherry tomatoes sautéed in olive oil and garlic, paprika, cilantro, house bread.
- **Pizza Trufona** — $10.500 — wild mushrooms in red wine, truffle oil.
- **Pizza Chanchito azul** — $10.500 — blue cheese, serrano ham, garlic oil.
- Pizzas: thin dough, homemade pomodoro sauce, mozzarella.
